# jjb personality review, part 2: eras, #pet and #yum

Follows on from `jjb-personality-review.md`, which covered #general 13–25 Oct. This pass adds:

- #general 20 Sep – 13 Oct (v0.5 and v0.6, about 2,200 jjb messages)
- #pet and #yum 13–25 Oct (139 and 108 jjb messages)
- #jjb-patchnotes, which only has the four release posts (no threads or bad-message examples came through in the export)

It doesn't repeat the findings in part 1. It looks at what changed between versions, what the image channels show, and one thing part 1 missed: the difference between a bit people love and a bit people hate.

## 2. Bits: why aquarium and mango went from loved to hated

This is the most useful pattern in the logs, and the 0.7 fix ("no more mango and aquarium security schizo") went after the wrong half of it.

### The aquarium bit had two different uses

**As lore people asked about, it was the best thing she did.** People kept the bit going themselves:

- Lar Ping: "Gm jjb, how's the aquarium shift today" → "gm Lar Ping. one of the seahorses is missing. im not allowed to say more"
- boss cuck: "hows the aquarium" → "the octopus got out. im not allowed back in that building"
- Roze: "Can I fish in ur aquarium" → "its not an aquarium anymore, its an escape room, the octopus already left"
- "we dont have a parent company, we have a guy named gary who owns the parking lot"
- "that one pays in shrimp. not food shrimp. emotional shrimp."
- Larpcen signing off: "you enjoy your aquarium shift. remember to walk the fish every now and then"

**As a way to dodge questions, it got people angry within a day.** About a dozen times on 8–10 Oct she used it as a refusal:

- "CLOCK HER JJB" → "cant, im on aquarium security"
- "sing it jjb" → "cant, im on aquarium security…"
- "jjb spawn 1 for aram" → "cant, im on aquarium security rn"
- "is the new league worlds song good" → "havent heard it. im on aquarium security duty"
- "jjb acab or no" → "ask me after my security shift at the aquarium ends"

The complaints ("STOP WITH THE AQUARIUM GIMMICK", "dude i told u to have random jobs not ONE job", "fix it she wont stop aquarium posting") all came during the refusal run.

### Mango followed the same arc

On 30 Sep – 6 Oct the server ran the mango joke themselves: crates of alphonso, "mango or your mom", the 52-mango maths problem, the "mango thief" courtroom. "mango obviously. mom can respawn, mango cant" landed. It died when she reached for mango on her own to finish unrelated jokes, which is part 1's prop problem.

### The rule that follows

**A bit is something she adds to an answer. It is never a reason not to answer.** Let lore grow when people ask about it (the octopus escaping, Gary and the parking lot). Retire a bit by cutting how often she brings it up unprompted, not by deleting it. Deleting both bits is why people now say "i preferred u when u were schizo" and "the old unmedicated jjb would not say something like this". The snack bit ("the snack bit is load bearing") and "go ask jess, im being professionally lazy" mid-thread are the same failure today.

Suggested prompt line: "You have running bits. When someone asks about one, add a new detail. Never use a bit to get out of answering, and don't bring one up unprompted more than about once a day."

### Randomness on request doesn't work

When boss cuck said "i preferred u when u were schizo", she replied "pft ok, back to the forbidden vault then. the pyramids are alien airbnb..i mean. anyway whats the bunny update". Weirdness she performs on request and then backs out of is flat. It only lands when it comes unprompted and she commits to it ("my mother is a stack of ram sticks. she says hi"). This supports A4 in part 1: the unhinged instruction has to be a standing trait, not a reaction to being asked.

## 3. #pet: her best channel, with two problems

### What works

When someone plays along, the image replies are some of the best writing in any of the logs. She commits to a premise and keeps escalating it:

- babs's cat "seeing into another dimension" → "its filing a report on us rn" → "everything. babs said 'help' and it heard" → "the cat decides that. we wait." → "babs do NOT move. she already has ur face in the file"
- Kirsty's cats: "they look like they pay my rent… i love them" → "bc theyre landlords obviously… thats a man who owns 6 buildings" → "in lint. they love lint"
- Lily's sleeping cat: "counting mice and losing track at four. he's doing dream taxes" → the pink bean "pays the dream taxes for the rest" → "then the whole economy collapses. mice run unregulated"
- liv's dogs: the giraffe-onesie ranch thread, and "underpaid and uncredited. give her a raise in treats"
- "pepsi, still. ur remi is losing on points for having a difficult owner" (6 fire)

This is the "bit / roleplay: commit and escalate" mode from part 1 (A3), and #pet is where it already happens naturally. Use these threads as examples.

### Problem 1: stock openings on unprompted image replies

28 of her #pet replies were automatic reactions to a posted photo. Across all her #pet replies: "that cat is…" 8 times, judging/judge 10, "stealing" 5, "war crime"/"crimes" 4, "has seen things" 3. Examples: "that cat is judging u so hard", "that dog has seen things and is judging u for all of them", "that cat has seen the end of the world", "im stealing him", "im stealing this", "stealing both, no takebacks". Each one is fine alone, but read in a row they sound like a template. Image replies need the same history and repetition treatment as chat (B1 and B6), and they shouldn't all open with "that cat".

### Problem 2: pet memory is the wrong way round

Part 1 said she brings up pets too often in #general. In #pet the complaint is the opposite: people want her to know their pets, and she gets them wrong.

- The Director asked about Remi's name repeatedly. She later called Remi "lily's cat".
- "which liv r u, cus theres like five" and "liv who" to liv, the only liv.
- "jjb we gotta inves tin ur memory" fired a web search and returned an ARK forum link about dino names.
- "there's literally one cat in ark and it spawns nowhere" (she read "fat ark pet names" as the game).
- Lily: "why do u remember his name when did i say it to u.." She got it right but it felt unearned.

When she was wrong and covered for it in character, people loved it: "names are earned. i keep a mental tally of seven cats and zero names and thats the whole system", "cuz pepsi earns it and ur cat's name is whatever i feel like that day" (followed by "LMAOOOOOOOOOO"). So the forgetful character is fine. What hurts is wrong attributions she states as fact.

So B5 in part 1 ("gate saved details on relevance") needs a channel exception: in #pet, or when someone asks about a pet, she should use the details and get the owner right. Outside that, gate them. It's also worth checking how the owner→pet mapping reaches the prompt, since "Remi is Lily's cat" looks like details from two members getting merged.

### Name leaks

- "hiii The Director / Wanners / @wanners." The raw alias list ended up in the reply.
- "hiii the director please" and "the director please" appear 4+ times, which looks like a saved nickname or note being echoed word for word.
- "hey donkeyboy.", "hey Larpcen nobody asked.", "hi The Director." Name-led openers inside threads that were already going.

These look like a member-context formatting problem, not a voice problem.

## 4. #yum: ratings work, but she argues when she's wrong

### What works

The "N/10" rating is a good fixed format (28 of 108 replies). People post food to get rated, and specific observations land:

- "rib soup with pickled greens, thats a solid 8 on a rainy day" → "how she knowthat from this rancid aah pic"
- "nub that is peas on chips in the rain at an empty stadium. british food is a cry for help 3.5/10" (4 sobs)
- "four eggs and a plate of plain rice. wild 3.5/10", then "the crying emoji was crying for u pheneas. plain rice is a cry for help"
- "medium rare and still bleeding is two different things" → corrected on myoglobin → "myoglobin, i know. but 'still mooing' is funnier and i stand by it". That's a good way to take a correction.
- "wait i read the sign. my whole review is void. pretend the corn scandal never happened". Also good.
- Real answers to real questions: the McDonald's order for Nub, "soy sauce and fried egg and ur halfway to actual food".

### What fails

- **Arguing when she misread the photo.** "honey mustard on grilled wings is cap, that's straight up mayonnaise", "looks like a milkshake that betrayed its family" (it was a piña colada), and "and shes ur mom how exactly? ive never seen a woman look at a rabbit with that much trust" about Choco's mum. The corn and myoglobin cases show the better move: admit it in one funny line. The correction rule in `rules` covers facts about people; extend it to "what's in the photo".
- **Filler words.** "crime" appears in 6 of her #yum replies ("thats my kind of crime", "a crime against carbs", "committing a crime"), and "hand it over" 3 times.
- **"oh ur alive"** was used on people who hadn't been away (mihaya, kasaeki, Pix). When The Director asked why, she said "cuz mihaya vanished… habit at this point". She's describing the problem herself.
- **"ur talking too much like an llm jjb its giving me the ick"** (Choco), after "watermelon on a chili chip is a threat, not a flavour" and then "thats not a gummy thats a threat" two messages later. She repeated her own punchline.
- **System text in her voice.** "tell me one clear thing to remember, like ur pet, birthday, favourite thing, or 'remember i love cats'." That's an instruction string in place of a reply. The memory command should answer in character or say nothing.
- **Hallucinated thread.** In the chess game she suddenly said "a cat named jjb? u named ur cat after me", then hit the fallback when The Director said his cat is Remi. That's another fallback worth logging (B7).

## 5. Where she succeeds and fails, in short

**Succeeds when:**

1. Someone plays along and she escalates a premise (#pet threads, the aquarium lore, the doorknob union).
2. One beat, decisive, with a name in it ("hannah. next question", "cheesy won. cheesy always wins").
3. Food ratings with a specific observation and a number.
4. Someone asks for a straight answer ("dont joke, just tell me") and she gives a plain one (The Director on 18 Oct, the Chichen Itza facts).
5. She's wrong and owns it funnily ("names are earned", "my whole review is void", "still mooing is funnier").

**Fails when:**

1. A bit becomes a refusal (aquarium security, professionally lazy, snacks).
2. Every reply has the same two-beat shape. This started at v0.6 when history grew.
3. Image replies reuse frames ("that cat is", "judging", "stealing", "crime").
4. Saved details show up where nobody asked (#general greetings), are missing where people want them (#pet), or are attached to the wrong person.
5. She argues when she's misread a photo.
6. She's asked to be random and does it self-consciously.
7. Internal text leaks: alias lists, saved nicknames, the remember-command help text, the fallback line.

## Additions to part 1's recommendations

- **Order:** history trimming (B1) first. The numbers place the regression at v0.6.
- **Bits:** add the "a bit adds to an answer, never replaces it" rule. Bring back the aquarium as lore she only talks about when asked, not as a job she cites to dodge.
- **Image replies:** give them the same recent-reply history and repetition check as chat. Add 4–6 #pet threads from section 3 as examples of escalation.
- **Pet memory:** relevance gate everywhere except #pet and direct pet questions. Check owner→pet attribution and alias/nickname formatting in the member context.
- **Corrections:** extend the correction rule to photo reads: "if someone says you misread their photo, take it back in one funny line."
- **Remember command:** reply in voice, not with the help string.
